# Bug ledger

One row per upstream defect found by this program: the reproducer, the compiler, the guilty pass or
rewrite, the state, the commit of the fix. Nothing enters without a minimal reproducer and a verdict
from the semantics.

States: `found` (reproduced here, not yet reported), `filed` (issue or PR open), `confirmed` (a
maintainer acknowledged it), `fixed` (an upstream commit), `wontfix`.

Tools referred to in the "found by" column:

- **validator**: `ttsem/validate.py`, the per-pass validator that names the culprit pass.
- **minimiser**: `ttsem/minimize.py`, the delta minimiser (`--reads` and `--crash` variants).
- **race detector**: `ttsem/races.py`, level 3.
- **layout fuzzer**: the companion differential fuzzer over layouts, dtypes and descriptors.
- **program fuzzer**: the companion generator of well-formed SIMT and tile programs with a
  lock-step interpreter as oracle.
- **LLVM continuation**: the companion lock-step interpreter of the LLVM dialect and NVVM, which
  takes over at the first `llvm.func`.
- **AxisInfo oracle**: the runtime oracle and transfer-function sweep for Triton's `AxisInfo`.
